LastwordInformation for estate planning counsel

An online tool your client configures, and the instrument controls.

One page. Prototype on NEAR testnet, November 2026. Not legal advice.

What it is

A RUFADAA-style online tool that the client configures. The client names heirs and shares, chooses the chains, and sets the proof-of-life interval, working from the distribution the will or trust specifies. The contract executes that configuration when the client stops sending heartbeats. It does not interpret or amend the governing document.

It is non-custodial. No party, including the developers, holds the client’s keys or can move assets outside the configured plan. The plan is public on-chain, so a fiduciary can read exactly what was configured.

What it is not

Not a will, trust, codicil or beneficiary designation, and it has no legal effect of its own. Not a custodian, escrow agent or fiduciary. It does not detect death: it acts on the absence of heartbeats, so the interval and grace period should suit the client’s circumstances. Assets must sit at the vault’s derived addresses to be included. It runs on NEAR testnet today, and there has been no third-party audit.

Referencing it in a trust
Sample. Review with counsel.

Digital asset distribution tool. The Settlor may configure an online tool known as Lastword (the “Vault”) to hold or distribute digital assets. The Trustee is authorized to rely on the distribution configured in the Vault as of the date of the Settlor’s incapacity or death, to the extent it is consistent with this instrument, and to access, manage, and transfer on-chain assets associated with the Vault as necessary to administer the trust. In the event of any inconsistency between the Vault’s configuration and this instrument, this instrument controls, and the Trustee may take such steps as are needed to give effect to it.

Security model
  1. Two narrow keys: the pulse key can only send a heartbeat, the agent key can only pay inside a stated policy. Neither can change heirs, amounts or destinations.
  2. Nothing executes while heartbeats arrive. Settlement opens only after the interval and the grace period have both passed.
  3. Anyone may run settlement, and no one may redirect it: the contract builds every transaction from the stored plan.
  4. Keys for other chains do not exist until the trigger; NEAR’s MPC network signs at settlement. The pulse and agent keys are deleted during settlement.
  5. Every Critical and High finding of the code reviews was fixed, each contract fix with a named regression test. The contract account has no full-access key, so nobody, its authors included, can change the code.
